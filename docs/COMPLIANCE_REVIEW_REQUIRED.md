# Legal and clinical review required before production

Qualified Iraqi legal, privacy, medical, pharmacy, laboratory, employment, tax, and telecommunications advisers must decide: lawful bases and consent; guardian/dependent access; medical record retention/amendment; clinical scope and supervision; e-prescription validity; controlled medication rules; lab verification; telemedicine consent; emergency wording/numbers; provider credential rules; cross-border processing; vendor contracts; breach duties; marketing consent; payment/tax/invoicing; record export/deletion; and minors/deceased patients.

The software must not claim HIPAA, GDPR, Iraqi-law, or other compliance until those decisions are implemented, evidenced, and audited.
